## 3. Regulation — City of Florence Ordinance 2023-22 (adopted 7/10/2023), Chapter 5 Article IX
Sources: florenceal.org ordinance PDF + STR application PDF
- STR defined as rental for fewer than 30 consecutive days; STR unit capped at 4 bedrooms.
- Required per unit: STR permit + certificate of occupancy + city business license; annual (calendar-year) renewal.
- Application requires: current room rates, deed, Lauderdale County Health Dept approval if septic, passed Florence Fire Marshal inspection, guest occupancy agreement, vacation rental property insurance, proof of state/county/municipal tax compliance, designated Local Contact Person (must respond to city notice within 30 minutes), no special events/parties/private functions.
- Permitted zoning: R-1, R-2, R-3, R-B, B-2 (residential uses 2nd story+), B-3, Sweetwater District, PUD (needs Planning Commission + Council). Historic districts require Board of Zoning Adjustment approval.
- TOT/lodging taxes apply to stays 30 days or less (platforms may remit; verify with AL DOR, city, county).
- KEY ARBITRAGE: stays of 30+ consecutive days are NOT STRs under this ordinance (treated as long-term residential rental) — a monthly furnished lease model avoids STR permitting, occupancy caps, and TOT exposure. Ordinance explicitly does not apply to hotels/motels/inns/B&Bs/long-term rentals.
- Sheffield, Tuscumbia, Muscle Shoals, unincorporated county: no STR ordinance found in public sources as of 2026-09-30 — verify directly before operating.
