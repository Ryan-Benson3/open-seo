## Executive Summary

1. **Total Shoals short-lease market ≈ $4.0–7.5M/yr gross room-night/furnished-rent revenue**, of which nightly STR is 70–80% ($3.5–6.1M across provider frames) — confidence M/H.
2. **Active STR supply is 94–220 listings depending on provider frame** (reconciled working range ~150–220 multi-platform active). Entry is accelerating (+34.8% to +105% YoY per two providers) while AirDNA's stricter "active" count fell −12.7% — consistent with an influx of amateur listings alongside professional consolidation.
3. **Reconciled STR benchmarks:** occupancy 35–50%, ADR $150–185, RevPAR $52–82/night, revenue per listing $19–28K/yr. The median operator earns ~$19–22K/yr; top quartile $34K+; top decile $48K+ — execution quality, not asset count, drives returns.
4. **3BR houses are the STR sweet spot** (best RevPAN $55/night, $20.5K/yr [Rabbu]); 4BR carries high ADR ($280) but collapses to 17% occupancy; 2BR is commoditized at $114 ADR.
5. **Zero branded extended-stay product within 50 miles** (no WoodSpring/MainStay/Everhome/Suburban; only independent Studio 6 Florence) — the single strongest structural gap in the market.
6. **Mid-term furnished supply is thin and fragmented** — single-digit examples per channel, no professional operator; the de facto competitors are Airbnb monthly-stay listings and Furnished Finder.
7. **Pricing ladder inversion:** hotels run $93–154/night (≈$2,800–4,600/mo) while observed furnished monthly asks are $750–1,200 — a mid-term product at $1,200–2,000/mo undercuts hotels by 40–70% and still yields 1.5–2.5× traditional rent (1BR avg ~$806/mo).
8. **Regulatory arbitrage:** Florence Ordinance 2023-22 defines STRs as <30 consecutive days; 30+ day furnished leases are exempt from STR permits, 4BR caps, and TOT exposure. Sheffield, Tuscumbia, and Muscle Shoals show no STR ordinance in public sources (verify directly before operating).
9. **Seasonality is the pure-nightly P&L killer:** Jan–Feb revenue trough of $878–1,027/mo vs June peak $2,392/mo [Rabbu]. A hybrid model (nightly in May–Aug + Oct peaks, 30+ day leases in the trough) is the natural structure — a 3BR leased at ~$1,700/mo for 12 months matches the average 3BR STR's annual revenue ($20.5K) with contracted occupancy and no TOT.
10. **Top risks:** ADR softening (−13.1% YoY [AirDNA]) amid supply influx; mid-term TAM sizing is low-confidence (derived from fragments, and provider data is OTA-invisible for off-platform channels); regulation outside Florence unverified.

### 3.6 Regulation as a competitive dimension [Ordinance 2023-22]

- Florence: STR = rental **<30 consecutive days**, capped at 4 bedrooms; per-unit STR permit + certificate of occupancy + business license, annual renewal; fire inspection, STR insurance, tax-compliance proof, 30-minute-response local contact; permitted zoning R-1/R-2/R-3/R-B/B-2/B-3/Sweetwater/PUD; historic districts need BZA approval; TOT applies to stays ≤30 days.
- **The 30-day line is the strategic fault:** a monthly-furnished-lease model is a long-term residential rental under the ordinance — no STR permit, no occupancy cap, no TOT exposure (verify platform remittance and AL DOR/city/county treatment).
- Sheffield, Tuscumbia, Muscle Shoals, unincorporated county: no STR ordinance found as of 2026-09-30 — a first-mover regulatory advantage pending direct verification. AirDNA Regulation sub-score 67/100 (moderate) reflects the Florence regime only.

### 4.2 Why they diverge (methodology, stated in pack + standard practice)

1. **Platform scope.** AirDNA deduplicates Airbnb+Vrbo+Booking.com; AirROI is Airbnb-centric; Rabbu counts Airbnb-listed units only. Multi-platform whole-market frames mechanically show more listings and more revenue.
2. **"Active" definition.** AirDNA's −12.7% vs Rabbu's +105% are not contradictory: AirDNA appears to prune dormant/occasional listings from the active count (consolidation at the professional end), while Rabbu's growth is off a small base (~46 → 94) counting newly listed Airbnb units. Gross entry is up; the professionally active core is not growing proportionally.
3. **Occupancy definition.** Occupancy is booked-nights-over-*available*-nights for some providers and booked-nights-over-*calendar*-nights for others. Blocked/owner-use nights sit in the denominator of the latter only. This alone can explain most of the 55% → 31% spread.
4. **Fee treatment.** ADR may or may not amortize cleaning/fees; typical fee-inclusion differences move ADR $20–40 — consistent with the $149 vs $185 gap.
5. **Window.** Rabbu's April snapshot misses the summer peak captured by the two TTM windows; Rabbu's own monthly curve (Jun $2,392) shows what it is missing.
6. **Internal-consistency check (each provider is arithmetically coherent, so the conflict is framing, not error):**
   - AirDNA: $149 × 55% = $81.95 ≈ RevPAR $82 ✓
   - AirROI: $185 × 37.5% = $69.4 ≈ RevPAR $67 ✓ (small definitional slippage)
   - Rabbu: $168 × 31% = $52.1 = RevPAN $52 ✓; $19,102/yr ÷ 12 = $1,592 ≈ $1,591/mo ✓
7. **One internal flag:** AirROI's headline mean ($18,962 = $1,580/mo) sits *below* its stated median ($1,802/mo). Revenue distributions are normally right-skewed (mean > median); mean < median implies a heavy left tail of near-inactive listings dragging the average — treat AirROI averages as understated and lean on its tiers instead.

### 5.2 Ranked opportunities for Bobo Group

1. **30+ day furnished leasing as the core engine** (Florence + the three unregulated cities). Rationale: exits the Florence STR regime entirely (no permit/cap/TOT) [Ordinance 2023-22]; prices in the $47–73/night-equivalent band sit under every hotel ($93+) and above every observed fragment ask; faces no branded or professional competitor. Blended targeting: nurses (13-week cycles), industrial crews (3–12 months), UNA-related stays.
2. **Hybrid 3BR houses — nightly in peaks, leased in the trough.** The 3BR is the proven RevPAN leader ($55, $20.5K/yr avg [Rabbu]). Break-even math: 12 months × $1,700/mo lease = $20.4K ≈ the average 3BR STR's entire year — with contracted occupancy, no vacancy risk, no TOT, no permit; the cost is forfeited peak upside (Jun market avg $2,392/mo). Season-swap models capture both.
3. **Travel-nurse-ready spec.** Only 53% of local listings have a workspace and 39% allow pets [Rabbu]; kitchen/parking/washer are near-universal but workspace + fast WiFi + in-unit laundry + flexible 13-week terms is an underspecified bundle. Channel: Furnished Finder + nurse FB groups (already routing demand here).
4. **Cheap differentiation in nightly STR:** hot tub (7% penetration), waterfront/lake access (11–16%) on the leisure side; exact-location display (only 37.6% show it) for trust.
5. **Avoid:** building a median-quality nightly 2BR portfolio. Bottom-quartile operators earn $967/mo at 20% occupancy [AirROI] while ADR is falling (−13.1% [AirDNA]) and new supply keeps arriving (+34.8%/+105% [AirROI/Rabbu]).

### 5.3 Risks & open verification items

- **Supply-trend conflict unresolved at the data level** (§4.2) — direction of competitive pressure in nightly STR is uncertain, another reason to anchor in the 30+ day segment.
- **Mid-term TAM is a derived estimate** (~$0.3–1.6M) and OTA-centric providers undercount off-platform channels in both directions; validate by calling Furnished Finder listings and the 4 Sheffield corporate units before underwriting.
- **Regulation:** verify Sheffield/Tuscumbia/Muscle Shoals + county directly; confirm TOT treatment and platform remittance with AL DOR/city/county; note Florence zoning whitelist (R-1…B-3, Sweetwater, PUD) and historic-district BZA approval if buying inside Florence.
- **Concentration risks:** NAMC contract cycles and Shoals EDA project timelines can shift; statewide $14.6B investment figure is not Shoals-specific.
- **Seasonality floor:** Jan–Feb revenue runs at ~37–43% of June [Rabbu] — any nightly-exposed unit must be lease-convertible in winter.

## 6. TL;DR

1. The Shoals short-lease pool is worth **~$4.0–7.5M/yr**; nightly STR is $3.5–6.1M of it across 150–220 listings, reconciled to ~42% occupancy / ~$165 ADR / ~$22K per listing.
2. Execution quality dominates: top-decile operators earn ~$48K vs the median's ~$20K, and **3BR houses are the only size class with best-in-class RevPAN ($55/night)**.
3. The exploitable whitespace is **30+ day furnished leasing**: zero branded extended-stay within 50 miles, fragmented amateur mid-term supply, and a $47–73/night-equivalent price band with no professional competitor between fragment asks and the $93+ hotel floor.
4. Florence's 30-day ordinance line makes the monthly-lease model **permit-free and TOT-exempt**, and the three other cities currently have no found STR ordinance at all (verify first).
5. Recommended shape: hybrid 3BR houses — nightly in the May–Aug/Oct peaks, ~$1,700/mo leases through the Jan–Feb trough — matching the market's average 3BR annual revenue with contracted occupancy and no tax/regulatory overhead.
