# Bobo Group — Shoals entry strategy

Analyst: Hermes Adam (operator / unit economics). Date: 2026-09-30. Grounded only in `research/shoals-stl-data-pack.md`. No new market scrape.

**Client assumption:** proposal specifics were not in the pack. This treats Bobo as a family group deciding how to put money into Shoals stays of a night through about six months. It is an operating plan, not a claim about houses they already own.

**How the cases are built (so the tables are not mixed):**

- **STR base** is the published 3BR comp, used as published: **$20,469/year, 32% occupied, $171 ADR, $55 RevPAN** [Rabbu]. Not AirDNA’s $27.5K. Not a rebuilt occupancy.
- **STR upside** is the AirROI top-25% **threshold**, labeled upside, not a forecast: **$2,824+/month, 58%+ occupied, $210+ ADR** [AirROI]. Revenue used is $2,824 × 12 = **$33,888**. Those three hurdles are not multiplied together (58% × $210 × 365 would invent ~$44K and is not used).
- **MTR base and MTR upside** are priced from furnished and hotel comps in the pack. They are not STR revenue with a new name.

Cost lines under the revenue anchors (cleaning, utilities, fees, insurance, furnish cash) are **assumptions**. They are not in the pack. Confidence on those plugs: low. Confidence on the published revenue anchors: high.

## Exec summary

- Do not buy at the published average home value (**$349,130**) and do not master-lease at the rents in the pack. After a normal mortgage, every scenario below — including both upside cases — has negative cash flow [Rabbu].
- STR base is a known, weak listing: **$20,469, 32% occupied, $171 ADR** [Rabbu]. After assumed operating costs, about **$7,000** is left before anyone pays for the house. That does not cover a mortgage or even an $806 1BR lease.
- STR top-quartile is a separate upside case (**$2,824/month** to merely enter the top 25%) [AirROI]. It is not the budget. A new operator in a market Rabbu scores **50/100** should not plan on it [Rabbu].
- The 30+ day product is a different business. Base ask is **$1,800/month**, built as the **$1,200** furnished 1BR asking plus a stated **$600** house premium, and checked against Stonebrook’s **$975** 2BR and hotel months of about **$2,790–$4,200** [Trulia, apartments.com, Choice]. It is not an STR rate in disguise.
- At that base ask and an assumed **75%** lease-up, MTR leaves about **$8,900** a year before housing cost — better than STR base, still not enough to pay market rent for a 3BR.
- Enter only if the family **already controls** a house for well under **$740/month** (free and clear, or a related-party lease). Then run **30+ day furnished leases, not nightly stays**. Florence does not treat 30+ consecutive days as a short-term rental, so the permit, the bedroom cap, and lodging tax do not apply [Ordinance].
- If a normal 12-month tenant will pay in the same **$1,200–$1,800** band and pay their own utilities, take that tenant. Less work. The pack has no 3BR long-term rent, so this is a check, not a fact.
- Go to market at **$1,800**, sold as two people at about **$900 each** (next to the $806–$975 apartment comps). Treat **$2,400** as an upside test against hotel shoppers, not as the price on the flyer.
- Channels are a price test: Furnished Finder and nurse groups first, corporate direct second, Airbnb/Vrbo only at a **30-night** minimum. In Florence, 28 nights is still a short-term rental [Ordinance].
- One unit. **75-day** kill. No second unit unless guests actually pay the upside price or the next house is just as cheap to control. Cap the year at two units. Confidence is high on the ordinance line and on the $20,469 anchor. Confidence is low on the $600 premium and on 75% MTR occupancy. That is why this is a test, not a fund.

## 1. Recommended product mix

**Lead with a conditional 30+ day furnished lease. Do not lead with a 3BR nightly rental. Do not lead with rent arbitrage.**

Florence’s rule is the product constraint. A short-term rental is a stay of **fewer than 30 consecutive days**. A stay of **30+ consecutive days is not an STR**. It is a long-term residential rental. That path avoids the STR permit, the certificate of occupancy, the city business license, the annual renewal, the fire-marshal inspection, the 30-minute local-contact rule, the party ban as a permit condition, the 4-bedroom cap, the zoning list, and lodging tax on stays of 30 days or less [Ordinance]. Hotels, motels, inns, B&Bs, and long-term rentals are outside the ordinance. Sheffield, Tuscumbia, Muscle Shoals, and the unincorporated county: no STR ordinance found as of 2026-09-30. That means “not found.” Verify with each planning desk before operating [Ordinance].

“Arbitrage” here means that regulatory line. It does **not** mean lease a place at Stonebrook prices and sublet it. Section 2 shows that trade loses money on the base case.

### What to run in year 1

| Share of nights | Product | When it is allowed | Why |
|---|---|---|---|

Prefer 60–90. | Only if the house is already controlled below the base-case ceiling in section 2 (about **$740/month** to break even, about **$340/month** if the family wants $400 left for their time). | Only product that beats STR base on leftover cash and skips the permit stack. |
| **0% of the plan** | Nightly or weekend 3BR STR | Not in the operating plan. A permitted Florence STR is a second business. Revisit only after a unit has actually hit the upside bar in section 6. | Published 3BR result is $20,469 at 32% occupied [Rabbu]. That does not pay for the house. |
| **0%** | 1BR, as STR or as MTR core | — | 1BR STR does **$15,549** at 29% occupied [Rabbu]. Furnished 1BR asking is already **$1,200**; Stonebrook 1BR is **$750** [Trulia, apartments.com]. Quality Inn is **$93–$100**/night [Choice]. No gap worth a company. |
| **0%** | 4BR, hot tub, event house | — | 4BR ADR is $280 but occupancy is **17%** and revenue is $31,205 — worse RevPAN than the 3BR [Rabbu]. Hot tubs are on **7%** of listings [Rabbu]. Florence bans parties on STRs [Ordinance]. A family group should not own that risk. |

**Unit spec, if a cheap-control house exists:** 3BR, two bedrooms that lock, parking, washer, kitchen, a desk, working AC, self check-in. Sleeps 4–6, cap the lease at 6. Kitchen, parking, self check-in, and washer are table stakes (95%, 93%, 87%, 82%) [Rabbu]. The gaps worth copying are **workspace (53%)** and **exact location (37.6% of listings show it)** [Rabbu, AirROI]. Match or beat **27.7 photos** [AirROI]. Skip the hot tub.

**Why 3BR even though nightly is not the plan:** it is the least-bad STR size if a unit is ever permitted ($55 RevPAN, best in the size table) [Rabbu], and it is the only MTR sales line that fits the comps. One person will not pay $1,800 when a furnished 1BR asks $1,200 [Trulia]. Two people at about **$900 each** sit next to the $806 average 1BR and Stonebrook’s $975 2BR [apartmenthomeliving, apartments.com]. That split is the offer. A crew of two to four is the same floor plan, and it is upside, not the base guest. Statewide investment figures and the Shoals spec building are real context; they are not mobilized demand inside this 12-month window [pack §4]. Do not put crews in the base occupancy.

**Lease paper:** one term of 30, 60, or 90 consecutive days. Weekly payment is fine. Weekly tenancy is the trap — a city can call rolling weeks an STR. Airbnb’s “monthly” setting often starts at 28 nights. **28 is still under 30. In Florence that is an STR** [Ordinance]. Any OTA minimum is 30 nights until a permit exists. Get a local lawyer to confirm auto-renew and whether an OTA checkout still counts as an STR before a Florence unit goes live. This memo is not that opinion. Confidence the clean 30+ day lease sits outside the ordinance: high, because the pack states it. Confidence on gray renewals: low.

**HOAs:** not in the pack. Ask anyway. A ban on leases under six months kills the product even when the city allows it.

**What not to propose:** a 5-to-15 listing Airbnb portfolio, a master-lease fund, or a purchase at the $349,130 average. AirDNA’s market score of **97** and its **$27.5K** average sit next to a regulation subscore of **67** and an ADR drop of **13.1%** [AirDNA]. Rabbu’s ROI score is **50**, with supply/demand “below average” [Rabbu]. Underwrite the 50, not the 97.

### 2.7 What has to be true to enter at all

1. The house is already controlled, or can be controlled, for **under $740/month** all-in to the owner (under **$340** if the family wants to be paid for their hours).
2. Guests will sign **30+ days at $1,800 or better**. If the market caps out at $1,200, do not operate.
3. The lease is a real 30/60/90, not rolling weeks, until a lawyer says otherwise [Ordinance].
4. Nobody is counting on AirDNA’s **$27.5K / 55% / score 97**, or on being in AirROI’s top 25% in year 1 [AirDNA, AirROI].

If (1) or (2) fails, the recommendation is **do not enter**. A plain long-term tenant is the better use of a house that can get one. The pack does not show that 3BR long-term rent; check it before furnishing.

## 3. Pricing strategy by season

Two different cards. Do not copy STR’s winter collapse onto the monthly lease.

What the pack says about seasons: revenue peaks **May–August and October**, trough **January–February**. Rabbu months: June **$2,392**, July $2,061, May $2,027, October **$1,950**, January **$878**, February $1,027 [Rabbu]. AirROI peaks August, March, and October; lows January, February, and April. Peak-month ADR **$202**; worst-month ADR still **$172** [AirROI]. March is not confirmed by both sources. Do not build a year on March.

Winter’s STR problem is empty nights, not a collapsed rate (worst-month ADR still $172) [AirROI]. Slashing toward bottom-quartile ADR of **$112** will not fill January [AirROI]. It cheapens the house. The MTR card does not follow that ADR. It follows the comp ladder in section 2.

### MTR card (the product in the plan)

Quote a monthly sum. On Furnished Finder you may show “about $420/week, 30-night minimum” ($1,800 ÷ 30 × 7). The contract is still 30, 60, or 90 days. Do not publish a nightly rate on a Florence listing. Nightly framing invites an STR label [Ordinance].

| Window | Months | Ask | What you are doing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Card rate, most of the year | Mar–Oct, except a tested peak bump | **$1,800** | The comp-built base. Two people at about $900. Do not open at $2,400. |
| Trough fill | Nov–Feb | **$1,600** for a signed **90-day** term; do not go under **$1,500** | Still above the $1,200 furnished 1BR asking and Stonebrook’s $975 [Trulia, apartments.com]. You are buying a winter lease, not matching Rabbu’s $878 January. A filled January at $1,600 is the product working. |
| Peak test | May–Aug, October | Hold $1,800. Test **$2,000** only if the first inquiries support it. | June STR revenue of $2,392 is a monthly *result* for a nightly listing, not a rent to copy [Rabbu]. Renaissance Faire (October) and summer festivals are not a reason to break a 90-day lease. |
| Upside, only after proof | Any season, after one lease has cleared | **$2,200–$2,400** | $2,400 is the upside column in section 2. It stays under Quality Inn’s $2,790 month and far under ~$4,200 [Choice]. It is not the list price on day one. |
| Term discount | Any | Floor **$1,600** on a guaranteed 90-day; never below **$1,500** | A signed 90 days at $1,700 beats the base expected collection of $1,350 (75% × $1,800). Take certainty. Below $1,500 you are back at the 1BR asking and the model is dead. |

Do not price against a hope of hotel capture in month one. The guest who is comparison-shopping Comfort Suites at ~$4,200 is the upside buyer. The guest who is comparison-shopping Stonebrook is the base buyer, and they only work if two of them split the house.

Lead time on the STR data is **41 days** [AirROI]. Market the next MTR opening **45 days** before the current guest leaves. Listing the week they move out is how a winter gap happens.

## 4. Channel plan

The job of the channels in year 1 is to find out whether anyone will sign **30+ days at $1,800 or better**. It is not to fill a nightly calendar.

Sell a furnished residence with a minimum term. Do not lead with “vacation rental.” The guest who fits the base price is a person on a contract, or two of them splitting $900. The music-trail weekend guest is the STR market that already has **94–220** listings and weak occupancy [Rabbu, AirDNA, AirROI].

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Furnished Finder** | Primary. Pack confirms FF serves Florence. | About **40%** of occupied nights, if any nights happen | Exact address. ### Kill-criteria

1. **No house under $740/month to control, and none free and clear, within 30 days of the October screen.** Stop. Do not buy at $349,130 [Rabbu]. Do not lease at $806, $975, or $1,200 hoping the upside column shows up [apartmenthomeliving, apartments.com, Trulia].
2. **No signed 30+ day lease at $1,600 or better within 75 days** of listing, after one $200 cut and real outreach (FF, nurse groups, and at least 10 direct contacts). Stop. The price test failed.
3. **Best offer is $1,200 or below.** Stop. That is the furnished 1BR asking, and section 2.6 leaves about $320/month before the house [Trulia].
4. **Any Florence stay under 30 consecutive days without a permit.** Stop that calendar the same day. Ordinance 2023-22 is in force [Ordinance].
5. **Two damage claims or neighbor complaints in six months.** Pause. Do not add a unit.
6. **Anyone proposes opening nightly bookings “so the year still works.”** Reject it. STR base is the worse leftover, and STR upside is a top-quartile result, not a rescue plan [Rabbu, AirROI].
7. **No second unit** unless unit 1 has done all three for 90 days: achieved rent **≥ $2,200**, occupancy **≥ 85%**, and the next house also clears the $740 control-cost screen. Missing any one of those, the year ends at one unit or at zero.
8. **Operator time over 6 hours a week after month 3.** Cap at one unit. Do not hire. A hire consumes the labor line the base case does not have.
9. **Do not raise the buy box because the upside column looks better.** MTR upside still loses about **$368/month** at the published home value after mortgage and tax [Rabbu]. STR upside loses about **$498/month** on that same buy.
10. **A 12-month unfurnished tenant at or above the MTR expected collection ($1,350), with tenant-paid utilities, is available.** Take the tenant. Furnishing a house to match a normal lease is the wrong proposal. Confidence this tenant exists: low — the pack has no 3BR long-term rent. Check before buying furniture.

**Upside gate (the only way the second column becomes an operating fact):** one unit, 90 days, rent at or above **$2,200**, occupancy at or above **85%**, utilities inside the plug, no ordinance miss. Until that gate, the upside columns in section 2 are labels, not a budget. AirROI’s top-25% bar ($2,824/month, 58%+ occupied, $210+ ADR) stays a market statistic, not a Bobo target [AirROI].
