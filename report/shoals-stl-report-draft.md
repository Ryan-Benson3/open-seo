## 1. Market snapshot

The Shoals (Florence, Muscle Shoals, Sheffield, Tuscumbia — Lauderdale + Colbert counties) is a small, infill-type short-stay market with genuinely conflicting headline numbers depending on provider methodology:

| Metric | AirDNA¹ | AirROI² | Rabbu³ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active listings | 220 (−12.7% YoY) | 186 (+34.8% supply) | 94 Airbnb-only (+105% YoY) |
| Avg occupancy | 55% (+17.5 pts YoY) | 37.5% | 31% (AL avg 38%) |
| ADR | $149 (−13.1% YoY) | $185 | $168 (AL avg $247) |
| RevPAR | $82 (+7.4% YoY) | $67 | $52 (RevPAN) |
| Avg annual revenue/unit | $27.5K (+64.7% YoY) | $19.0K | $19.1K |

¹ TTM Aug 2025–Aug 2026, Airbnb+Vrbo+Booking.com deduplicated. ² Aug 2025–Jul 2026, Airbnb-centric. ³ Snapshot Apr 27 2026, Airbnb listings only.

**Read-through:** the truth is a ~90–220 unit market (provider counts differ on channel coverage and dedup) earning roughly **$19–27K/year per active unit at 31–55% occupancy and $147–185 ADR**. Even the bearish view shows revenue per unit growing and supply consolidating, not flooding. AirDNA's composite Market Score is **97/100** (investability 97, demand 92); Rabbu's ROI score is a far more cautious **50/100** on below-average supply/demand balance. Both can be true: cheap entry prices with soft occupancy = a market that rewards the right unit, not any unit.

**Performance tiers (AirROI):** top decile units earn $3,996+/mo at 75%+ occupancy and $319+ ADR; median units $1,802/mo at ~38%; bottom quartile $967/mo at ~20%. Execution quality swings revenue ~4x.

**Seasonality:** peak May–Aug (June best ≈ $2,392/mo avg) with a distinct October secondary peak (≈$1,950 — events/football/fall travel); trough Jan–Feb (≈$878/mo). Roughly a 2.7x spread between best and worst month.

**What wins here:** 3BR houses (32% of supply, best RevPAN $55, $171 ADR, 32% occ, $20.5K/yr avg). 4BRs earn the most gross ($31.2K/yr, $280 ADR) but fill poorly (17% occ). 2BRs fill best (35%) but price weakly ($114 ADR). Supply is 98% entire-home, 73% houses, average unit sleeps 5.1 — a family/group market, not a solo-business-traveler market.

## 2. Competitive landscape

**A. Branded extended-stay: zero.** No WoodSpring Suites within 50 miles (nearest AL: Huntsville, Madison, Prattville). Choice Hotels' extended-stay brands return "0 properties" for Florence. Nobody operates an institutional weekly/monthly product in the Shoals.

**B. Hotels (nightly anchors, Oct 2026):** Quality Inn Florence East $93–100; Comfort Inn Tuscumbia $112–124; Comfort Suites Florence Shoals $139–154; Marriott Shoals Hotel & Spa anchors the premium tier. A 30-night stay at Comfort Suites' rate ≈ $4,200+ — far above what a furnished house should price at monthly.

**C. STR/Airbnb supply (competitor set above):** ~94–220 units, fragmented, mostly amateur hosts (avg 27.7 photos but only 37.6% show exact location — thin merchandising discipline).

**D. Furnished monthly / mid-term: fragmented and thin.** A handful of apartment-complex furnished units (Stonebrook Village Muscle Shoals $750 1BR/$975 2BR; Alpine Village corporate units), scattered $1,200/mo 1BR furnished askings on Trulia, 4 "corporate apartments" in Sheffield, aggregator listings (corporatehousing.com), Airbnb/VRBO monthly-stay pages, and Furnished Finder + Facebook travel-nurse groups absorbing nurse demand one house at a time (e.g., 1010 River Road, Muscle Shoals).

**E. Traditional rentals (the floor):** ~$806/mo average 1BR in Muscle Shoals ($450–$1,300 range). Furnished short-term product prices 1.5–3x this floor.

**Structural takeaway:** the market has a barbell — amateur nightly STRs and 12-month unfurnished apartments — with a nearly empty middle: professionally run 1–6 month furnished housing. That middle is exactly where demand signals (below) concentrate.

## 3. Regulation (City of Florence Ordinance 2023-22, adopted 7/10/2023)

- STR = rental **< 30 consecutive days**; max 4 bedrooms/STR unit; permit + certificate of occupancy + business license per unit, renewed every calendar year; Fire Marshal inspection; vacation-rental insurance; local contact who can respond to the city within 30 minutes; no parties/events; allowed in R-1, R-2, R-3, R-B, B-3, Sweetwater, PUD (and B-2 above ground floor); historic districts need BZA approval. Lodging/TOT applies to stays ≤ 30 days.
- **The arbitrage: stays of 30+ consecutive days are not STRs under the ordinance at all** — no STR permit, no occupancy cap, no TOT exposure; treated as ordinary residential tenancies. A monthly-furnished product gets hotel-adjacent revenue with residential-class compliance burden.
- Sheffield, Tuscumbia, Muscle Shoals, unincorporated counties: no published STR ordinance found as of 2026-09-30 — verify with each municipality before operating. AirDNA's regulation sub-score (67) and AirROI's "low regulation" both flag light-touch oversight.

## 4. Demand drivers

- **Healthcare (strongest repeatable signal):** North Alabama Medical Center anchors regional care; travel-nurse and contract-clinician demand is active enough that Furnished Finder, travel-nurse Facebook groups, and DSCR lenders all specifically work the Florence market. Typical 13-week contracts sit squarely in the empty mid-term band.
- **University:** UNA enrolls 10,204 students — athletics, family visits, commencement, camps; also staff/faculty transitional housing.
- **Music-heritage tourism:** FAME Studios, Muscle Shoals Sound, Alabama Music Hall of Fame; W.C. Handy Festival (summer), Renaissance Faire (October) — matches the observed June and October revenue peaks.
- **Industrial/construction:** Alabama's record 2025 ($14.6B, 9,388 jobs statewide); Shoals EDA actively marketing the Florence Advantage Site and a 150,000 sq ft spec building — project crews need 3–12 month housing and currently have nowhere professional to land.
