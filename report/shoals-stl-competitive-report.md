## 1. Market snapshot

The Shoals (Florence, Muscle Shoals, Sheffield, Tuscumbia — Lauderdale + Colbert counties) is a small short-stay market whose headline numbers depend heavily on provider methodology:

| Metric | AirDNA¹ | AirROI² | Rabbu³ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active listings | 220 (−12.7% YoY) | 186 (+34.8% supply) | 94 Airbnb-only (+105% YoY) |
| Avg occupancy | 55% (+17.5 YoY) | 37.5% | 31% (AL avg 38%) |
| ADR | $149 (−13.1% YoY) | $185 | $168 (AL avg $247) |
| RevPAR | $82 (+7.4% YoY) | $67 | $52 (RevPAN) |
| Avg annual revenue/unit | $27.5K (+64.7% YoY) | $19.0K | $19.1K |

¹ TTM Aug 2025–Aug 2026, Airbnb+Vrbo+Booking.com deduplicated. ² Aug 2025–Jul 2026, Airbnb-centric. ³ Snapshot Apr 27 2026, Airbnb-listed only. Fourth source (guestfavorites, Sep 2026): $25.2K/unit, 52% occ — inside the band.

**Reconciliation (all four agents, independently):** each provider is internally consistent (ADR × occupancy ≈ RevPAR in all three), so the conflict is framing, not error. Platform scope ("active" definition, Airbnb-only vs multi-platform) and occupancy denominators (available vs calendar nights) explain most of the spread. Two of three providers cluster low (31–37.5% occ, ~$19K/unit); AirDNA is the optimistic outlier whose +64.7% revenue gain alongside −12.7% listings and −13.1% ADR is a **survivorship signature** — weak listings exited and the survivors' averages rose. **Underwrite to the low cluster; treat AirDNA as the ceiling achievable only with top-decile execution.**

Working bands: **occupancy 35–50%, ADR $150–185, revenue $19–28K/yr per active unit**, total nightly-STR pool **$3.5–6.1M/yr** across ~150–220 listings; add a derived, low-confidence **$0.3–1.6M** mid-term furnished segment.

**What the supply mix says:** 98.4% entire homes, 72.6% houses, 3BR is the modal listing (32.3%), average unit sleeps 5.1 — a family/group leisure market with a value price ceiling (ADR 32% below state average). Performance is execution-driven: top decile $3,996+/mo at 75%+ occ and $319+ ADR vs bottom quartile $967/mo at ~20% — a ~4x spread on the same market. By size (Rabbu): 3BR is the sweet spot ($171 ADR, $20.5K/yr, best RevPAN $55); 2BR is commoditized ($114 ADR); 4BR earns high rates ($280) but fills only 17% — event inventory. Seasonality: June peak $2,392/mo avg, October secondary peak $1,950, January trough $878 — a 2.7x spread the fixed costs don't respect.

## 2. Competitive landscape

**A. Branded extended-stay: zero.** No WoodSpring Suites within 50 miles (nearest AL: Huntsville, Madison, Prattville); Choice's extended-stay brands return "0 properties" for Florence. The brands looked and passed — a density decision, not a legal ban (hotels are exempt from Florence's STR ordinance). What that gap is worth is a **small professional book, not a hotel**: an 8–15 unit mid-term portfolio ≈ $120K–$300K/yr gross; a branded 80–100 key box would need $1.8–2.3M of contracted room-nights nothing in the data supports.

**B. Hotels (nightly anchors, Oct 2026):** Quality Inn Florence East $93–100; Comfort Inn Tuscumbia $112–124; Comfort Suites Florence Shoals $139–154 (≈$2,790–$4,620 monthly equivalent); Marriott Shoals Hotel & Spa anchors premium. These set the mid-term price ceiling: a furnished lease near $1,800/mo saves a 13-week travel nurse $3,000–$7,200 vs hotel rack.

**C. Nightly STR supply:** ~94–220 fragmented, mostly amateur units (only 37.6% show exact location; 53% have a workspace; 7% hot tubs) — thin merchandising discipline, top of market winnable with professional execution.

**D. Furnished monthly / mid-term (the empty middle):** a handful of fragments — Stonebrook Village (Muscle Shoals, $750 1BR/$975 2BR), one Florence furnished ask at $1,200/mo, 4 "corporate apartments" in Sheffield, an unpriced Alpine Village corporate listing, aggregator pages, Airbnb/VRBO monthly-stay listings, Furnished Finder, and travel-nurse Facebook posts. **A channel list, not a product.** No professional operator; the $47–73/night-equivalent mid-term band has no competitor between fragment asks and the $93+ hotel floor.

**E. Traditional rentals (the floor):** ~$806/mo avg 1BR (Muscle Shoals) — furnished product prices 1.5–3x this floor, which is why the mid-term buyer is "temporary by the job, not by preference."

## 3. Regulation (City of Florence Ordinance 2023-22, adopted 7/10/2023)

- STR = rental **< 30 consecutive days**; max 4 bedrooms/STR unit; per-unit STR permit + certificate of occupancy + business license, renewed every calendar year; Fire Marshal inspection; vacation-rental insurance; local contact who can respond to the city within **30 minutes**; no parties/events; permitted zoning R-1/R-2/R-3/R-B/B-3/Sweetwater/PUD (B-2 above ground floor); historic districts need BZA approval. Lodging/TOT applies to stays ≤ 30 days.
- **The strategic fault line: stays of 30+ consecutive days are not STRs at all** — no permit, no occupancy cap, no TOT exposure; ordinary residential tenancies. The compliance moat is statutory.
- Sheffield, Tuscumbia, Muscle Shoals, unincorporated counties: no STR ordinance found in public sources as of 2026-09-30 — "not found" ≠ "none"; Florence's 2023 text is a ready-made template one council meeting away. Vacuums close. Verify each municipality before operating; monitor Florence for definitional amendments (e.g., a shift to 28 days) quarterly.

## 4. Demand map — who actually rents

| Segment | Stay | Side of 30-day line | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Contract clinicians (NAMC)** | ~13 weeks | Lease side | **Real, recurring, counter-seasonal — the anchor.** Furnished Finder + nurse groups actively serve the market; the only durable year-round driver. Magnitude unverified (no census): plan 10–25 concurrent, not hundreds. |
| UNA (10,204 students) | 1–4 nights; thin 2–8 week tail | Mostly STR side | Real but event-concentrated (~4–6 weekends/yr); explains May–Aug + Oct peaks; hotels absorb the top. Not a monthly book. |
| Music heritage tourism | 1–4 nights | STR side | Real short-stay driver with a value ceiling; peak-month occupancy still <50%. **Served — not the hole.** |
| Industrial crews (Advantage Site, 150K sqft spec building) | 3–12 months | Lease side | **Option value only** — EDA marketing, no tenant/timeline. One real project ≈ $160–190K over 6 months; do not underwrite until a shovel is in the ground. |
| Insurance / relocation | 1–8 weeks | Split | No local counts; surge optionality, not a build-for segment. |

Geography: demand concentrates in **Florence** (NAMC, UNA, industrial park, and the only STR ordinance — which is what makes 30+ day leases a distinct product). Muscle Shoals second (overflow + studios). Sheffield is the supply comp; Tuscumbia is event overflow.

## 5. Panel verdicts and divergence

| Analyst (model) | Angle | Bottom line |
|---|---|---|
| A — OMP · GLM-5.3 | Market landscape | $4.0–7.5M total short-lease pool; largest exploitable gap = 30+ day furnished leasing; hybrid 3BR (nightly peaks, leased trough) is the natural structure. |
| B — Pi · GLM-5.2 | Risk stress-test | Median nightly STR now nets LESS than a plain long-term rental (~$7.9K vs ~$12.4K on a $300K house); levered nightly STR is cash-negative in every scenario. The unbreakable play is the furnished 30+ day lease. |
| C — Hermes-Adam · Grok-4.7 | Entry strategy | Base case fails at market prices. MTR base NOI $8,880/yr (STR $7,044). Enter only with mispriced control: existing family-owned property, sub-market purchase, or control cost ≤ $340/mo. 12-month phased rollout with hard kill gates. |
| D — Hermes · Grok-4.7 | Demand map | Clinician housing near NAMC is the #1 unmet need; the mid-term shelf is empty ($120–300K/yr defendable for 8–15 units); do not build hotel-scale or another 4BR event house. |

**Where they diverged:**
- **Entry optimism.** A liked a hybrid nightly+lease model; B and C rejected nightly as a base-case load-bearing wall (keep it as an option); C went furthest — at the $349K ZHVI average, neither base STR nor base MTR pencils, so entry requires mispriced control or top-quartile execution. **Resolution adopted: lease-first structure, entry-price discipline as a gating condition (C's position), nightly revenue as peak-season optionality on proven units (A's structure).**
- **Own vs master-lease.** B: master-leasing at market rents clears only $250–400/mo/unit — thin; owning beats arbitraging. C: arbitrage works only ≤ $340/mo control. **Consensus: use existing/mispriced owned units; market-rate master leases are out.**
- **TAM framing.** A sized the whole pool ($4–7.5M); D restricted the *defendable* mid-term book to $120–300K. Not contradictory — A measured the ocean, D measured what a family group can actually land.

## 6. Consensus findings (all four agents)

1. **Median nightly STR is the wrong trade in this market.** Commodity 2BR/3BR nightly listings face a supply influx (+35–105% in the Airbnb channel), falling ADR, hotel-anchored price caps, and a Jan–Feb trough at ~40% of peak. The bottom quartile earns $967/mo.
2. **The 30+ day furnished lease is the opportunity.** Every analyst independently ranked it first: statutory exemption from Florence's STR regime, priced between fragment asks ($750–1,200) and hotel rack ($2,790–4,620/mo equivalent), anchored on the only durable year-round demand (clinicians), floored by the ordinary rental market if it fails.
3. **The competitive middle is empty.** Zero branded extended-stay within 50 miles; mid-term supply is a scrap pile of fragments with no professional operator.
4. **Underwrite conservatively.** Use the low data cluster ($19–21K gross/unit/yr equivalent, ~35% occupancy) — never AirDNA's 55%/$27.5K — and never count spec-building crews or insurance spikes in the base case.
5. **Florence first.** Demand and the regulatory clarity both concentrate there; keep units purely on one side of the 30-day line (a single sub-30-night stay can pull a Florence unit into the STR regime).

## 7. Recommendation for the Bobo Group proposal

**Product:** 30+ day furnished mid-term leases — 1–2BR nurse-grade units (desk, fast WiFi, in-unit laundry, kitchen, parking, pet-friendly option, invoiceable to staffing firms) near NAMC in Florence. 3BR houses only in a season-swap configuration: leased Oct–Apr, optionally nightly (permitted, taxed) May–Sep on proven units.

**Pricing:** list at **$1,800/mo** (band $1,600–2,000; upside test $2,200–2,400). Break-even for a travel nurse vs Quality Inn rack ≈ $3,000+ of savings per 13-week assignment; the binding ceiling is the cheap hotel, not the nice one.

**Unit economics to present (per unit, annual):**

| | Base | Upside (top-quartile ops) |
|---|---|---|
| MTR gross | $16.2–22.8K (75–90% fill) | $28.8K |
| MTR NOI | **$8.9–13.0K** | $18.2K |
| STR comparison NOI (3BR) | $7.0K | $16.7K |
| Control-cost break-even | $340–580/mo | $990–1,120/mo |

**Gating conditions (do not enter without one):** existing family-owned property, a sub-market acquisition, or control cost ≤ $340/mo. At the $349K market average with market-rate financing, the base case does not clear — B and C agree on this from independent math.

**Rollout (C's plan, adopted):** Month 0 confirm gating condition + verify municipal rules → Months 1–2 acquire/furnish one unit ($8–10K cap) → Months 3–4 list at $1,800 with a 75-day kill clock → Months 5–8 optimize pricing, test upside → Months 9–12 scale to a second unit only if proven. Kill criteria: no mispriced control found in 30 days, or no signed lease at $1,800 within 75 days of listing → stop.

**Verification checklist before the proposal is finalized:**
1. Call Sheffield, Tuscumbia, Muscle Shoals + both counties re: STR/rental ordinances (the "not found" is unverified).
2. Confirm TOT/platform remittance treatment with AL DOR + City of Florence for any sub-30-night stays.
3. Scan Furnished Finder for vacant $1,200 listings (if they sit empty, the clinician thesis weakens) and collect Studio 6 Florence's actual weekly rate (the unpriced budget competitor).
4. Contact NAMC staffing agencies — can they invoice/master-account? (The economic buyer may be the agency, not the nurse.)
5. Do not underwrite Florence Advantage Site / spec-building crews until a tenant and timeline are announced.
